# Walmart Same-Day Double-Sided Business Cards near 72714 — Verdict

**As of 2026-08-04 · Researcher: web-puppy-305f83 · For: granlund-grove business card proof run**

## Straight answer: NO.

Tyler cannot walk into a Walmart near Bella Vista today and walk out with 50 double-sided, full-bleed business cards from his own PDF on decent stock. Not at any NWA store, not for any price.

### The three hard blockers (all confirmed on Walmart's own site)

1. **HOME DELIVERY ONLY.** The Walmart Photo business card product page states *"How you'll get this item: Home Delivery"* — the ONLY fulfillment option. Business cards are not printed in-store; they are fulfilled by a third-party trade printer and shipped. Walmart's own "same-day" documentation lists the same-day catalog as *"one-hour photo prints and passport photos; same-day gifts such as canvas prints, posters, photo books, and calendars; and same-day cards"* (greeting cards) — **business cards are not in it.** Estimated arrival for non-same-day items is "usually 3–5 days."

2. **NO PDF UPLOAD.** The closest thing to custom is the *"Upload Your Design"* product — but that means uploading **JPEG/PNG images into the online layout tool**, one face per image. There is no PDF, vector, or bleed-spec intake anywhere in the flow. Tyler's 2-page print-ready `granlund-card.pdf` (3.75"×2.25" with bleed, embedded Fraunces/Inter fonts) cannot be submitted as-is. He would have to rasterize each face and fight a template editor.

3. **FRONT-ONLY.** The entire business-card catalog (24 designs, product `2423-business-card`, 3×2 theme) is built around a single printed face — themes like "Photo Window", "Name Tag", "Full Photo" are one-sided photo-card products. Nothing on the product page, in "Key Features", or in the theme pack indicates a double-sided option. Tyler's charcoal-front + QR-back design has no path.

### Gotcha check (full bleed / heavy dark coverage)

- The "Full Photo" theme does print edge-to-edge — so bleed-ish output exists — **but only on the front**, and only by uploading a flattened raster into their crop tool (their crop box, not your bleed marks).
- Stock is **120 lb cardstock with a "natural matte finish"** — that's a genuinely decent weight for the price, but uncoated natural matte + a near-full-coverage charcoal solid on a photo trade printer's digital press is exactly where you get washed-out blacks and banding. No laminate, no coating, no rich-black control, no option to specify anything.
- Template editor implies a safe margin; your own trim/bleed geometry is not honored.

### In-store copy/print counter check

Walmart has **no walk-up document/business printing counter** (no FedEx-Office-style service). The Photo Center does prints/posters/passport photos/same-day gifts; "Print Services" as a copy-and-print counter does not exist at Walmart. Photo kiosks were removed from most stores years ago; remaining same-day output is dye-sub photo prints and poster/gift items. NWA stores (Bella Vista, Bentonville, Rogers, Centerton) are Supercenters with Photo Centers, but none of that matters here because **business cards never touch the store** — they're shipped from the trade printer.
