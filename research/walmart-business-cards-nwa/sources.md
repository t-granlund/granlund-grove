## Methodology notes / access issues
- Google and Bing SERPs both served captchas; DuckDuckGo served a bot challenge. Worked around by hitting Walmart's official photo storefront (photos3.walmart.com, the live Photo Center backend behind walmart.com/photo) directly via curl with a browser UA — **all four sources above are primary/official**.
- walmart.com/photo itself bot-blocked the headless browser ("Robot or human?"), but redirects to the same photos3.walmart.com backend, which served full HTML including pricing, fulfillment method, and stock specs server-side (extracted from raw HTML).
- The legacy `prints.walmart.com` portal is NXDOMAIN (decommissioned) — consistent with migration to photos3.walmart.com.
- "No double-sided option" and "no PDF upload" are negative findings: no mention of duplex printing, back-side design, or PDF/vector upload anywhere in product page, key features, theme pack slugs (all single-face photo-card themes), or category copy. Confidence: high but flagged as absence-of-evidence; the single-face theme architecture + front-only marketing ("Full Photo", "Photo Window") corroborates.
- NWA store Photo Center hours not re-verified this session (Walmart store pages bot-blocked); immaterial to the verdict since business cards never route to stores.
