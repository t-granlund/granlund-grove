# Local Print Shops for Premium Dark Business Cards — Bella Vista / NW Arkansas (72714)

**Research date:** August 4, 2026 · **Prepared by:** web-puppy-c126b1

## The Job (from project context)

- **Trim:** 3.5"×2" · **Supplied file:** 3.75"×2.25" full-bleed print-ready PDF (`dist/card/granlund-card.pdf`, 2-page front/back, fonts embedded)
- **Design challenge:** rich **charcoal full-coverage background** (needs excellent dark-fill coverage; thin quick-print stock will fail)
- **Stock target:** 16pt minimum, **32pt preferred**, matte / soft-touch
- **Quantity:** proof run of **25–50**, with comparison pricing for 100 / 250
- **Both sides printed**

## Headline Recommendation

For **this specific premium dark card**, the strongest options in order:

1. **FedEx Office (Bentonville — 1406 S Walton Blvd, ~5.4 mi)** — *Best combination of PDF-friendliness + premium stock.* Their upload spec **explicitly asks for 3.75"×2.25" files with bleed** (exactly your PDF), and they carry **130 lb soft-touch** (velvety matte — ideal for dark designs) plus 240 lb ultra-thick. Same-day / next-day pickup. **This is the safest bet for a same-week proof run from your exact file.**
2. **Broadaway Printing (Rogers, ~9.2 mi)** — *Best for premium/offset quality on a rich charcoal fill.* Established local commercial printer with an online **"Send a File"** PDF upload + custom-quote form, 100/120 lb gloss & matte cover with **aqueous coating**. Call for a 32pt/soft-touch option and a 25–50 proof price.
3. **Staples Print & Marketing (Rogers, ~8.3 mi)** — *Fastest/cheapest same-day*, 14/16/38pt matte or gloss, PDF upload with bleed line, but max published stock is 38pt "ultra thick" and digital dark-fill can band. Good for a cheap test proof, not the final premium run.

**Gotcha to flag everywhere:** digital presses (all the chains) can show **banding/mottle on large dark solid fills** and the charcoal can look washed-out on light/uncoated stock. **Soft-touch or matte lamination** both masks banding and deepens the black. Always request a **physical proof** before the 250 run.
