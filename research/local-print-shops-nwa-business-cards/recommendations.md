## Phone script (to get comparable quotes fast)
> "I have a print-ready 2-page PDF, 3.75×2.25 with bleed, trimming to 3.5×2, full-coverage charcoal background both sides. Can you quote **25, 50, 100, and 250** on your **heaviest matte or soft-touch stock** — do you have **32pt or soft-touch/suede**? And can I get a **physical proof** of the dark fill before the full run?"

## Dark-card gotchas (verify with whoever prints)
- Confirm they will print from **your PDF**, not force a template rebuild.
- Ask for **matte/soft-touch lamination** — it masks digital banding and deepens charcoal.
- Avoid light/uncoated stock; it can't hold a rich black.
- Get a **physical proof** (project bc-12) and iPhone-scan the QR from the actual printed stock before committing to 250 (ties into the existing bc-14 proof-run-of-25–50 plan).

## Bottom line
- **Fastest + best PDF match:** FedEx Office Bentonville.
- **Best premium/charcoal quality:** Broadaway (then Morpho) — call for 32pt/soft-touch + proof.
- **Cheapest bulk check:** Staples Rogers 16pt matte ~$27.99/250.
