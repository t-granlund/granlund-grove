## Tier 1 — Official / primary sources

### National chains (product, stock, pricing, upload spec)
- **FedEx Office — Business Cards** — https://www.fedex.com/en-us/printing/business-cards.html
  - Verified claims (extracted live 2026-08-04):
    - Upload spec: *"Your file should measure 3.75" × 2.25" to"* include bleed; *"Preferred format: .PDF"*, 300 dpi+, design extends to edge of bleed.
    - Stocks: *"100 lb. matte cardstock… 130 lb. white cardstock… 130 lb. soft-touch cardstock (smooth, velvety coating)… 240 lb. ultra-thick cardstock (rigid… luxury)."*
    - Pricing: Quick 3.5"×2" Single **$15.00/100**, Double **$18.75/100**; Premium 3.5"×2" Single **$19.99/100**, Double **$39.99/100**. "Starting at $0.15 each" (Quick) / "$0.18 each" (Premium).
    - Turnaround: *"Most Quick Business Cards are available for same-day or next-day pickup… in-store pickup."*
  - Credibility: Tier 1, primary vendor, current.

- **Staples — Business Cards / Print & Marketing Services** — https://www.staples.com/services/printing/business-cards/
  - Verified claims (extracted live 2026-08-04):
    - Stocks: *"standard 14pt, premium 16pt or ultra-thick 38pt paper stock in matte or gloss."* Specialty: laid/linen/smooth.
    - Pricing: Standard 14pt matte **$21.99/250**; Premium 16pt matte **$27.99/250**; Ultra thick 38pt **$49.99** (for 250).
    - Upload: *"you may upload files in the form of a JPG, PNG or PDF… minimum 300 DPI and color mode of CMYK… We trim your business cards between the safe area and the bleed line."*
    - Turnaround: *"Order by 12 pm for same-day service"* (traditional cards); same-day in-store pickup on select items.
  - Credibility: Tier 1, primary vendor, current.
